# NVIDIA (gaming)

Gaming runs on the **NVIDIA RTX 5070** with proprietary driver `595.84`
([../hardware/gpu.md](../hardware/gpu.md)).

- Vulkan works; the card is detected correctly.
- **Avoid unnecessary driver changes** — regressions here affect both gaming and
  the Wayland desktop.

Historical NVIDIA/Vulkan issues (now resolved):
[../troubleshooting/solved-issues.md](../troubleshooting/solved-issues.md).
