# NVIDIA (gaming)

Gaming runs on the **NVIDIA RTX 5070** with driver `595.71.05` (open kernel
module; [../hardware/gpu.md](../hardware/gpu.md)). Install method and version
pin: [../operating-system/nvidia-install.md](../operating-system/nvidia-install.md).

- Vulkan works; the card is detected correctly.
- **Avoid unnecessary driver changes** — regressions here affect both gaming and
  the Wayland desktop.

Historical NVIDIA/Vulkan issues (now resolved):
[../troubleshooting/solved-issues.md](../troubleshooting/solved-issues.md).
